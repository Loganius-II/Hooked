~~ON RAFT~~

- press 1 to get fish rod
- click in water to fish

- hover cursor over backpack and press number keys to display items

 - press wasd to move

- click on map to set destination

~~COOKER~~
- Drag a rusty boot or wet news paper into the fuel slot 
- drag a fish into the food slot and it will cook the food, giving you more hunger points

~~WHILE ANCHOR UP~~
- A and D to steer
- DO NOT DARE TO SAIL AWAY FROM THE MAP

~~ISLANDS~~
- Walk up to different booths to display the Menu Uis for them
- walk away to close it 
- press E to exit island

~~FINISHING THE GAME~~
